## Itemized bills
Patients can ask the provider for an itemized bill that lists each service, supply and drug with its code and charge. Review the itemized bill before paying a disputed balance.

## Disputing a charge
Contact your health plan to dispute charges for services you did not receive or that were billed more than once. Write down the representative's name, the date and the reference number for every call.
