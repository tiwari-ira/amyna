## Required detail
Claims that use an unclassified drug code (for example HCPCS J3490) must identify the drug name, the National Drug Code (NDC), and the dose administered. Without this detail the payer cannot verify the charge.

## Member requests
A member may request an itemized statement that names each drug billed. Payers typically pend or deny unclassified drug lines until the provider supplies the invoice and NDC.
