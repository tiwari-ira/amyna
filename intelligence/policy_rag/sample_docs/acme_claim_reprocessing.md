## Requesting reprocessing by phone
Members or their authorized representatives may call member services to request reprocessing of a claim. The representative will verify the member's identity before discussing claim details and will issue a reference number for each request.

## Authorized representatives
An automated assistant may call on a member's behalf when the member has authorized it. Identity verification questions must be answered by the member directly.
