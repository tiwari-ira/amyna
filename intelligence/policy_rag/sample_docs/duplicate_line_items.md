## Identical lines on the same date
A claim line that repeats the same procedure or revenue code, the same date of service, the same units and the same charge, without a modifier that explains the repetition, is treated as a duplicate. Duplicate lines are removed during claim reprocessing.

## Facility fees
A single outpatient visit generates one facility fee for the encounter. A second identical facility fee for the same encounter requires documentation of a separate visit.
